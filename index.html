Questions about training with SportArc

Which sports work with Auto Cut?

Auto Cut supports tennis, badminton, and table tennis video.

Are AI Coach and Match Review available for every sport?

Auto Cut supports tennis, table tennis, and badminton. AI Coach and Match Review currently focus on table tennis.

What can I learn from Match Review?

For supported table-tennis matches, SportArc brings together technical data, tactical data, ball speed, placement, shot patterns, match phases, and key moments so you can understand more than the final score.

How is a SportArc AI training plan personalized?

The plan analyzes your goal, training focus, weekly frequency, session length, difficulty, available setup, the injury information you choose to provide, and every training or match activity you add to your Growth Profile.
