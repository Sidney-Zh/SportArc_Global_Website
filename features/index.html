Prepare the video before you start

Record the session with your phone, then import it into SportArc from Photos or Files. Check the view for your sport and feature before filming a full session. Match Review needs a fixed camera; AI Coach needs a clear view of the player’s movement.

How can SportArc help after training or a match?

Use Auto Cut to find and export rallies from tennis, badminton, or table tennis recordings for your own review or to share with a coach. For table tennis, use AI Coach for supported stroke analysis and Match Review for match data. Plus also includes personalized training plans for all three sports.

What do I need to record a session?

Start with a phone recording, then import it from Photos or Files. The camera position and visible action need to suit the feature: check the recording guide before filming a full session.

Does SportArc provide AI coaching for tennis and badminton?

AI Coach and Match Review currently support table tennis. Tennis and badminton support Auto Cut, and Plus offers personalized training plans for all three sports. A training plan is a separate feature from video-based technique analysis.
